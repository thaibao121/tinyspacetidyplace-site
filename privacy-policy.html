Privacy Policy

Tiny Space, Tidy Place · Effective date: November 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Tiny Space Tidy Place Publisher application ("the App") handles information when it interacts with Pinterest on behalf of an authorized user.

1. What the App does

The App publishes original pins (images and short videos) about small-home organization and compact kitchen storage to the Pinterest business account that authorizes it. It may also read that same account's boards and pins in order to publish and organize content correctly.

2. Data we access

The App uses Pinterest's official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. When you authorize the App, Pinterest grants it a token scoped to the permissions you approved (for example: read boards, write boards, read pins, write pins). The App only ever acts on the Pinterest account that granted authorization.

3. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect, store, or share personal data of any other Pinterest user. It does not sell data to anyone, does not share data with advertisers, and does not use Pinterest data for advertising or profiling.

4. Data retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored securely and used solely to keep the authorized connection working. Technical logs (such as request timestamps and error messages) may be kept briefly for debugging and are not shared with third parties.

5. Third-party services

Data that lives on Pinterest remains subject to Pinterest's Privacy Policy. This website itself does not use tracking cookies or analytics.

6. Your rights

You may revoke the App's access at any time from your Pinterest account settings (Settings > Security > Apps). Revoking access immediately stops the App from acting on your account.
