☕ Countertop Organization

In a small kitchen, the countertop is prime real estate — every square inch is either prep space or clutter. The goal isn't empty counters; it's intentional counters where everything left out earns its spot.

What to look for

  • Corral, don't scatter. A single tray or turntable groups daily items (oil, salt, coffee) into one movable zone instead of five scattered ones.
  • Go up, not out. Tiered stands and wall-mounted rails free the actual counter surface for cooking.
  • One in, one out. Before buying any countertop organizer, decide what leaves the counter to make room — organizers multiply what you keep visible.
  • Easy-clean materials. Counters collect grease and crumbs; smooth, wipeable surfaces beat fabric or raw wood here.
  • Appliance garages for the big stuff. If the toaster and coffee maker live on the counter, a dedicated station keeps them from sprawling.
